About Us

Discover the heart, mission, and vision behind Hope Lifting Centre Church.

Our Mission in Brief

Hover to read

Hope Lifting Centre Church exists to share the love of Jesus Christ, restore hope, strengthen families, and transform lives through faith, prayer, pastoral care, and community service. We are committed to supporting vulnerable individuals, empowering young people, caring for older adults, and providing spiritual guidance and practical support to those in need. Through compassion, integrity, and service, we seek to build a stronger, more inclusive community where everyone is valued, encouraged, and given the opportunity to flourish.

Our Purpose

Our Mission Statement

Hope Lifting Centre Church is a Christian faith-based organisation established to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, promote Christian worship and spiritual growth, and demonstrate God's love through practical service to individuals, families, and the wider community.

Our mission is to bring hope to the hopeless, support the vulnerable, strengthen families, empower young people, care for older people, and promote the spiritual, emotional, and social well-being of those in need.

Through Christian teaching, prayer, pastoral care, community outreach, advice and support services, counselling-related support, youth development programmes, and activities for older adults, we seek to improve lives, reduce social isolation, encourage personal development, and build a caring and inclusive community.

We are committed to serving people with compassion, dignity, integrity, and respect, regardless of their background or circumstances, while remaining faithful to our Christian beliefs and values.

Our Future

Our Vision Statement

Our vision is to become a Christ-centred church and community organisation that transforms lives through the love of God, strengthens families, empowers future generations, supports older people, and provides practical assistance to individuals experiencing hardship, isolation, or other life challenges.

We aspire to build a community where people can grow spiritually, develop their potential, experience meaningful relationships, and access appropriate support to improve their quality of life.

Our Aims & Objectives

Six pillars that guide our daily work and long-term goals.

A. Advancement of the Christian Faith

  • To advance the Christian faith through worship, prayer, preaching, biblical teaching, discipleship, and evangelism.
  • To organise church services, prayer meetings, Bible studies, conferences, and other Christian activities.
  • To provide pastoral care and spiritual guidance to individuals and families.
  • To encourage Christian values, compassion, forgiveness, integrity, and service to others.

A. Advancement of the Christian Faith

B. Community Support and Advice

  • To provide information, guidance, and practical support to individuals and families experiencing social, emotional, or personal difficulties.
  • To help individuals identify appropriate services and community resources.
  • To promote social inclusion, community participation, and stronger family relationships.
  • To signpost beneficiaries to qualified professionals and specialist agencies where their needs fall outside the organisation's expertise.

B. Community Support and Advice

C. Counselling-Related and Emotional Support

  • To offer appropriate pastoral listening, emotional support, encouragement, and guidance.
  • To support individuals experiencing loneliness, bereavement, family difficulties, and other personal challenges.
  • To refer individuals to appropriately qualified counsellors, healthcare professionals, or specialist services where necessary.
  • To ensure that any professional counselling services are delivered by suitably qualified and authorised practitioners.

C. Counselling-Related and Emotional Support

D. Youth Development and Empowerment

  • To provide positive opportunities for young people to develop confidence, leadership skills, and personal responsibility.
  • To organise mentoring, educational activities, life-skills sessions, workshops, and recreational programmes.
  • To encourage young people to make positive choices and participate constructively in their communities.
  • To promote the welfare, personal development, and social inclusion of young people.

D. Youth Development and Empowerment

E. Support for Older People

  • To reduce loneliness and social isolation among older adults.
  • To organise social gatherings, companionship activities, group discussions, and community events.
  • To promote dignity, inclusion, emotional well-being, and meaningful social connections.
  • To help older people identify relevant community resources and support services.

E. Support for Older People

F. Relief of Hardship and Support for Vulnerable Individuals

  • To provide appropriate practical support to individuals and families experiencing hardship, isolation, or disadvantage.
  • To signpost people to relevant welfare, community, and support services.
  • To promote equality of opportunity, human dignity, and social inclusion.
  • To work collaboratively with appropriate community organisations and service providers where this advances the church's charitable purposes.

More About Our Work

Click the tabs below to read about our community impact, volunteers, and governance.

Hope Lifting Centre Church seeks to make a positive and lasting contribution to the community by combining Christian ministry with practical community support.

Our activities are intended to strengthen family relationships, encourage young people, reduce isolation among older adults, support individuals experiencing personal difficulties, and help people access appropriate services.

We recognise the importance of working responsibly, maintaining appropriate safeguarding arrangements, and ensuring that our activities respond to genuine community needs.

Volunteers may contribute to the organisation's mission by assisting with church activities, community outreach, programme coordination, youth activities, older people's programmes, administrative tasks, and other appropriate voluntary activities.

All volunteers will be expected to follow the organisation's policies, code of conduct, and safeguarding procedures.

Where relevant, recruitment, supervision, eligibility checks, and role-specific training will be undertaken in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Any international volunteering arrangements will be subject to the relevant UK immigration rules. The organisation will ensure that any sponsorship undertaken is lawful and that individuals perform only activities permitted by their immigration status.

Hope Lifting Centre Church is committed to responsible leadership, good stewardship, appropriate financial management, and compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

The organisation will seek to ensure that its activities are conducted in furtherance of its stated purposes, that its resources are used responsibly, and that appropriate procedures are in place for safeguarding, record-keeping, risk management, and accountability.

Where the organisation undertakes activities involving children, young people, or adults at risk, appropriate safeguarding measures will be implemented.
